# **App Name**: Riyadh TowAssist

## Core Features:

- User & Driver Authentication: Secure registration and login functionality for both car owners and tow truck drivers.
- Location-Based Tow Request: Users can input their exact breakdown location on a map, explicitly selecting their district (e.g., 'شمال الرياض' (North Riyadh) and its sub-districts).
- Vehicle and Incident Details Input: Users provide details about their vehicle (make, model) and the nature of the problem (e.g., accident, flat tire, non-starting engine).
- Tow Truck Matching & Dispatch: Efficiently matches the user's request with available and nearest tow truck drivers, considering their location and truck capacity.
- Real-time Tracking: Allows users to track the assigned tow truck's real-time location and estimated arrival time after dispatch.
- Driver Panel (Basic): A simplified interface for tow truck drivers to accept requests, update their status, and navigate to the pickup location.
- AI Car Issue Descriptor Tool: An AI-powered tool that assists users in accurately describing common car problems or damage by prompting relevant questions.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design draws inspiration from concepts of reliability and urgent assistance, leaning towards a clean and professional aesthetic. The primary color is a strong, slightly desaturated blue (#1F4CB3), conveying trust and efficiency. This color choice balances immediate attention with a sense of calm professionalism.
- The background color is a very light grey-blue (#E8EDF2), which supports the primary color by being visually soft and ensuring high readability across the application. It creates a bright and airy feel, ideal for a functional service application.
- An accent color, a vibrant but balanced aqua-blue (#4DCAE0), is used for interactive elements and highlights to signify important actions or status changes, such as 'Accept Request' or 'Arrival Imminent.' This creates a clear visual hierarchy and draws user attention when necessary.
- For both headlines and body text, the font 'Inter' (sans-serif) is recommended. Its clean, neutral, and highly readable design ensures that critical information, such as locations and service updates, is always clear and easily understood by users, even in potentially stressful situations.
- Utilize modern, easily recognizable line icons for critical elements like vehicle types, location pins, tow trucks, and emergency services. Icons should be minimalist to ensure quick comprehension.
- The layout emphasizes clear, sequential steps for service requests, prominently featuring map views for location selection and real-time tracking. Information is organized with ample white space for visual clarity, reducing cognitive load during potentially urgent situations.
- Subtle and functional animations are incorporated to provide feedback for user interactions, such as request submissions, successful matches, and loading states, enhancing the user experience without causing distraction. Animations for tracking the tow truck should be smooth and intuitive.
